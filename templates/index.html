How does mental effort change the way you type?

A 5-minute study of keystroke rhythm. You get a personal result at the end, and your anonymous data helps research on cognitive load.

  1. Type normallyCopy a short passage at your usual pace.
  2. Type under loadCopy another passage while counting backwards by 7.
  3. See your resultCompare your rhythm across the two tasks.

What is recorded

Which keys you press and when (to the millisecond), the text you type, and your answers below. No name, email or IP address is stored. You get a personal result at the end, and you can close this page at any time to withdraw.

Use a physical keyboard on a computer. Phones and tablets are not supported.
